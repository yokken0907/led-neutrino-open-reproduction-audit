# Journal fit and claim scope

## Suitable framing

This work is a **partial/open computational replication and reproducibility audit** of published
LED-neutrino constraints using publicly accessible implementations and data products.

It is not framed as:
- discovery of LED / extra-dimensional physics;
- proof that the target papers are wrong;
- exact reproduction of JHEP Figures 5/6;
- exact replay of Elaçmaz et al.;
- proof that one specific likelihood choice is the sole cause of the discrepancy.

## ReScience C caution

ReScience C explicitly accepts partial replications and does not judge novelty, but a negative
replication that claims the original result is mistaken receives a substantially higher burden
of proof. This repository therefore does not use the label "non-replicable original result".
It documents what can and cannot be reconstructed from public materials.

Journal selection should remain open until the manuscript is complete.
